Who we are

What is ELECTRO?

ELECTRO stands for Education in Learning Electronics, Computer Technologies, and Real-World Opportunities. We run hands-on bootcamps so you do not just hear about engineering — you build it.

Electronics

You will read circuit diagrams, use a breadboard, then meet a real PCB — the solid board that replaces a tangle of jumper wires.

Computer tech

You already know a bit of Arduino. Today that skill drives motors and turns an ESP32 into a tiny Wi-Fi hotspot.

Real-world build

This is an individual project. No shared kit. You wire it, code it, decorate it, race it, and take the car, PCB, and ESP32 home.

The day

What we will do

One school day. Five stages. You leave with a working car if you follow the checkpoints and ask for help early.

01

Circuits

Symbols, series vs parallel, breadboard, then PCB.

02

H-bridge

Make a motor spin both ways. Then wire the PCB.

03

Code + Wi-Fi

Write applyDirection. Join the ESP32 hotspot from your phone.

04

Build

Assemble the chassis, mark your car, test in the safe zone.

05

Race

Cardboard / pipe track, timed runs, photos, and take-home.

Morning is circuits + H-bridge (about 9:30–12:20). Lunch is 12:30–1:00. Afternoon is code, build, and race (1:00–2:30), then wrap-up.
